What this is

A deterministic advisor over a parsed snapshot of MIT's Course 6 subject listings. Prerequisites are parsed into boolean expression trees and evaluated against your record — no language model is involved in anything on this page, so the same inputs always give the same answer.

Every eligibility verdict is one of three values. Eligible and missing prerequisites are computed. Can't verify means a requirement couldn't be read — an instructor-permission clause, or a GIR you haven't recorded. An unverifiable requirement is never rounded up to a yes.

What it does not know

  • Degree requirements. No GIRs, HASS, CI-H/CI-M, major, minor or concentration requirements are in this dataset. There is no progress bar here because there is no authoritative source behind one.
  • Other departments. Course 6 subject listings only. Prerequisites naming subjects outside Course 6 are shown but can't be looked up.
  • Communication requirements. The snapshot dropped every CI-M and CI-H designation before it was written — 6.UAT is a CI-M subject and carries no such marker here. Flagged, not silently accepted.
  • When it was current. The snapshot carries no fetch date, so offerings may be stale.
  • Instructors. Recovered by a text heuristic; some are missing or wrong.
  • 51 subjects have no description — jointly listed, with the text living under another department's number.
